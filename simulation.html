Newton's Third Law of Motion

Why Do Rockets Fly into Space?

Rockets don't push against the ground or air! Burning fuel pushes hot exhaust gas downwards with tremendous force (Action), which creates an equal and opposite force pushing the rocket upwards (Reaction).

🔻
1. The Action Force (Faction)

High-speed burning exhaust gas is expelled downwards through the nozzle.

🔺
2. The Reaction Force (Fthrust)

By Newton's 3rd Law, an equal and opposite upward thrust propels the rocket into the sky!
