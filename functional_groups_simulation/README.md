# Organic Functional Group Identification Virtual Laboratory

Interactive STEM organic qualitative analysis simulation modeling confirmatory test tube reactions across unsaturation, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids.

## Pedagogical Objectives (NCERT Class 12 Chemistry Practical)
1. **Test for Unsaturation ($C=C / C\equiv C$)**:
   - Bromine Water Test: Reddish-brown bromine solution de-colorizes immediately upon addition to unsaturated alkene/alkyne.
   - Baeyer's Test: Cold dilute alkaline $\text{KMnO}_4$ de-colorizes from bright purple to a muddy brown $\text{MnO}_2\downarrow$ precipitate.
2. **Test for Alcoholic Group ($-OH$)**:
   - Sodium Metal Test: Dry sodium metal piece placed in anhydrous alcohol yields brisk effervescence of hydrogen gas ($2\text{ROH} + 2\text{Na} \to 2\text{RONa} + \text{H}_2\uparrow$) tested with squeaky pop sound.
   - Esterification Test: Alcohol heated with glacial acetic acid and conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ yields characteristic sweet, fruity aroma of ethyl acetate.
3. **Test for Aldehydes ($-CHO$) vs Ketones ($>C=O$)**:
   - Tollens' Silver Mirror Test: Ammoniacal silver nitrate reduced to a sparkling, reflective metallic silver mirror coating the inner glass tube surface.
   - Fehling's Test: Equal parts Fehling A ($\text{CuSO}_4$) and Fehling B (alkaline sodium potassium tartrate) heated with aliphatic aldehyde yield brick-red cuprous oxide ($\text{Cu}_2\text{O}\downarrow$).
4. **Test for Carboxylic Acid Group ($-COOH$)**:
   - Sodium Bicarbonate Test: Vigorous brisk effervescence of carbon dioxide gas ($\text{RCOOH} + \text{NaHCO}_3 \to \text{RCOONa} + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2\uparrow$) turning lime water milky.
