📚 Resonance Tube & Speed of Sound Principles

1. Closed-End Organ Pipe Physics

A column of air enclosed over a water surface acts as a closed organ pipe. Sound waves reflected from the rigid water boundary interfere with incoming waves from the vibrating tuning fork to form standing waves.

Water boundary ⇒ Displacement Node (N)
Open tube mouth ⇒ Displacement Antinode (A)

2. Resonance Positions & End Correction (Lord Rayleigh)

Because the antinode extends slightly beyond the physical rim of the tube by an end correction $e \approx 0.3 \times d$ (where $d$ is tube inner diameter):

First Resonance (Fundamental): L₁ + e = λ / 4
Second Resonance (First Overtone): L₂ + e = 3λ / 4

3. Speed of Sound Determination

Subtracting the two equations eliminates the end correction $e$ entirely:

L₂ − L₁ = λ / 2 ⇒ λ = 2(L₂ − L₁)
Speed of Sound: v = f × λ = 2f(L₂ − L₁)
