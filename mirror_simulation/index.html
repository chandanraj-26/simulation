📚 Spherical Mirror Formulas & Cartesian Sign Conventions

1. New Cartesian Sign Convention

  • The pole $P$ of the mirror is taken as the origin $(0, 0)$.
  • The principal axis of the mirror is taken as the $x$-axis.
  • Object is always placed to the left of the mirror (incident light travels from left to right).
  • All distances measured to the right of the pole (along incident light) are positive (+).
  • All distances measured to the left of the pole (opposite to incident light) are negative (−).
  • Heights measured upwards perpendicular to principal axis are positive (+).
  • Heights measured downwards are negative (−).

2. The Mirror Equation

1/f = 1/v + 1/u ⇒ v = (f × u) / (u − f)

3. Linear Magnification (m)

m = −v / u = hi / ho
  • m < 0 (negative): Real and inverted image.
  • m > 0 (positive): Virtual and erect image.
  • |m| > 1: Magnified image.
  • |m| < 1: Diminished image.
