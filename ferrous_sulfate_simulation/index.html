🎯 NCERT Quests

📚 Chemical Equations & Theory (NCERT Chapter 1)

Stage 1: Dehydration (Loss of Water of Crystallization)
FeSO₄·7H₂O(s) ⟶ FeSO₄(s) + 7H₂O(g)

Hydrated green vitriol (pale green) loses 7 water molecules on mild heating (~100–300°C), turning into dirty white anhydrous FeSO₄. Droplets of condensed water appear on cooler upper tube walls.

Stage 2: Strong Thermal Decomposition
2FeSO₄(s) ⟶ Fe₂O₃(s) + SO₂(g) + SO₃(g)

On strong heating (>500°C), ferrous sulfate decomposes into solid ferric oxide (Fe₂O₃, reddish-brown) and two suffocating acidic gases: sulfur dioxide (SO₂) and sulfur trioxide (SO₃).

Acidic Gas & Redox Confirmation Tests
SO₂ + H₂O ⟶ H₂SO₃ (Turns Blue Litmus Red)

Both SO₂ and SO₃ are acidic non-metal oxides. They dissolve in the moisture of moist blue litmus paper to turn it red. SO₂ also reduces orange potassium dichromate to green chromium sulfate: Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 3SO₂ + 2H⁺ ⟶ 2Cr³⁺ + 3SO₄²⁻ + H₂O.
