📐 New Cartesian Sign Convention

According to the New Cartesian Sign Convention used internationally and across Class 9–12 physics textbooks:

  • The Optical Center (O) of the lens is taken as the origin (0, 0).
  • The Principal Axis corresponds to the horizontal X-axis.
  • Incident light always travels from Left to Right (+x direction).
  • All distances measured to the right (+x) are Positive (+).
  • All distances measured to the left (-x) are Negative (-).
  • Heights measured perpendicularly upward (+y) are Positive (+).
  • Heights measured perpendicularly downward (-y) are Negative (-).
