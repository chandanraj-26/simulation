Density & Floatation — NCERT Theory

1. Density Definition:
Density (ρ) is defined as mass per unit volume of a substance. Liquids with lower densities float atop liquids with higher densities, provided they are immiscible:

Density Formula:
ρ = m / V   (SI unit: kg/m³ or g/cm³)

2. Immiscibility & Density Stratification:
Vegetable oil is non-polar and hydrophobic, preventing it from mixing with polar water or dense honey. The five distinct liquid layers stably stratify in ascending order of density from top to bottom: Rubbing Alcohol (0.79) < Oil (0.92) < Water (1.00) < Dish Soap (1.06) < Honey (1.42 g/cm³).


3. Buoyancy & Equilibrium Floatation:
When a solid specimen is dropped into the tower, gravity exerts downward weight while displaced liquid exerts upward buoyant force:

Downward Weight: W = ρobj · V · g
Upward Buoyant Force: FB = ρliquid · Vsubmerged · g
Equilibrium Floatation: W = FB  ⇒  Specimen rests at boundary where ρliquid, top < ρobj ≤ ρliquid, bottom
