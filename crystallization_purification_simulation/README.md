# Purification of Impure Copper Sulfate by Crystallization Virtual Lab

An interactive STEM virtual chemistry laboratory simulating the standard NCERT / CBSE Class 9 & 11 practical technique for purifying crude copper sulfate ($\text{CuSO}_4 \cdot 5\text{H}_2\text{O}$) from insoluble sand/dirt and soluble impurities through dissolution, hot gravity filtration, concentration, and crystallization.

## Key Features
- **Authentic 5-Stage NCERT Practical Protocol**:
  1. **Dissolution**: Dissolve crude impure blue vitriol in minimum volume of distilled water with $2\text{–}3$ drops of dilute $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ (to prevent hydrolysis of $\text{Cu}^{2+}$).
  2. **Hot Gravity Filtration**: Filter hot solution through fluted Whatman filter paper in a glass funnel to remove insoluble sand and suspended dirt particles.
  3. **Concentration & Crystallization Point**: Heat clear blue filtrate in a porcelain china dish on sand bath until a crust/film forms when blown on a dipped glass rod.
  4. **Slow Cooling**: Cover dish with a watch glass and allow slow undisturbed cooling overnight at ambient room temperature ($25^\circ\text{C}$).
  5. **Separation & Drying**: Decant mother liquor, collect sparkling deep-blue triclinic copper sulfate pentahydrate crystals, press between filter papers.
- **Stoichiometry & Yield Engine**:
  - Live mass balance tracking: Initial Crude Mass ($10.00\text{ g}$), Insoluble Residue ($0.85\text{ g}$), Mother Liquor Loss, Pure Crystal Yield ($7.65\text{ g}$), and Percentage Yield ($\approx 83.6\%$).
- **Microscopic Triclinic Crystal Lattice Zoom**: High-resolution view of octahedral $[\text{Cu(H}_2\text{O})_4]^{2+}$ units linked by bridging sulfate ions and 5th uncoordinated lattice water molecule.
- **Soft Light-Blue Academic Aesthetics**: Compliant with study environment law, zero external dependencies.
