Refraction & Chromatic Dispersion

Why Does White Light Split into Rainbow Colors?

When white light enters a transparent glass prism, light doesn't just bend — it separates! Different colors travel at different speeds in glass. Violet light slows down the most (speed v = c / n) and bends the most, while Red light travels fastest and bends the least, revealing the magnificent VIBGYOR spectrum discovered by Sir Isaac Newton in 1666!

⚡
1. Surface 1 Refraction

Snell's Law: n₁ · sin(i) = n₂ · sin(r₁). Light bends toward the normal as it enters the denser glass medium.

🌈
2. Cauchy Dispersion

Because refractive index for violet is higher than red (nviolet > nred), each color bends at a different angle.

📐
3. Total Deviation (δ)

Emergent rays bend away from the normal into air, producing total deviation δ = i + e − A onto the observation screen.
