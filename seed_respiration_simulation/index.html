Biochemical Principles
Aerobic Respiration:
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Energy

KOH Chemical Fixation:
2KOH + CO₂ → K₂CO₃ + H₂O

Mechanism of Water Rise:
Volume of O₂ absorbed by germinating seeds is matched by CO₂ released, but that CO₂ is instantly absorbed by the KOH pellets. This lowers flask pressure (Pflask < Patm), pulling water up the tube.

Seed Respiration — NCERT Class 10 Practical

1. Aim of Experiment:
To experimentally demonstrate that carbon dioxide (CO₂) is released during the aerobic respiration of actively germinating gram/pea seeds.

Aerobic Cellular Respiration:
C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + 38 ATP (Energy)

2. Role of Potassium Hydroxide (KOH):
A suspended test tube inside the conical flask contains fresh KOH pellets. KOH absorbs and chemically fixes gaseous carbon dioxide:

CO₂ Fixation Reaction:
2KOH(aq) + CO2(g) → K2CO3(aq) + H2O(l)

Because the evolved CO₂ is trapped into potassium carbonate, the gas volume inside the airtight flask decreases by the volume of O₂ consumed. This creates a partial vacuum (Pflask < Patm), and atmospheric pressure forces colored water up the delivery tube.


3. Control Experiment:
When boiled seeds soaked in carbolic acid (antiseptic) are used as a control, respiratory enzymes are thermally denatured and cellular respiration ceases. No O₂ is consumed, no CO₂ is evolved, and the water level in the bent delivery tube remains completely unchanged.
