📖 Laboratory Manual: Solubility & Crystallization

1. Solubility Definition: The maximum mass of solute in grams that can dissolve in $100\text{ g}$ of a solvent at a specific temperature to form a saturated solution.


2. Three Solution States:

  • Unsaturated Solution: Contains less solute than the saturation limit at that temperature. More solute dissolves easily upon stirring.
  • Saturated Solution: Equilibrium between dissolved solute and undissolved solid. No more solute can dissolve at that temperature.
  • Supersaturated Solution: Contains more dissolved solute than normal saturation. Formed by cooling a hot saturated solution. Highly unstable; introducing a seed crystal triggers immediate crystallization!

3. Crystallization vs. Evaporation: Crystallization is superior to simple evaporation to dryness because it yields pure crystalline solids without decomposing heat-sensitive salts or concentrating soluble impurities.
