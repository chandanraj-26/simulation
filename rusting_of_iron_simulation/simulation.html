Electrochemical Rusting Mechanism

Rusting is an electrochemical process requiring both Oxygen and Water:

4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O

Anode: Fe(s) → Fe²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻

Cathode: O₂(g) + 2H₂O(l) + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻(aq)

Electrolytes (salts) accelerate electron transfer, increasing corrosion rate.
