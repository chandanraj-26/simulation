# Mendelian Genetics Cross & Punnett Square Virtual Laboratory (`/mendelian_genetics_simulation`)

An interactive STEM genetics virtual laboratory for NCERT Class 10 & Class 12 Biology exploring Gregor Mendel's Laws of Inheritance, monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, Punnett squares, and Monte Carlo statistical sampling.

## Key Features
- **Dual Genetic Cross Modes**:
  - **Monohybrid Cross**: Investigating plant height ($T = \text{Tall}$, $t = \text{Dwarf}$), revealing the classic $3:1$ phenotypic ratio and $1:2:1$ genotypic ratio.
  - **Dihybrid Cross**: Investigating seed shape ($R = \text{Round}$, $r = \text{Wrinkled}$) and seed color ($Y = \text{Yellow}$, $y = \text{Green}$), generating the landmark 16-cell Punnett square with the $9:3:3:1$ phenotypic ratio.
- **Monte Carlo Random Offspring Sampling Engine**: Generate batches of 10, 50, 200, or 1000 offspring in real-time, observing the Law of Large Numbers as random fertilization converges into the theoretical Mendelian proportions.
- **Botanical Illustrations**: Visual rendering of tall vs. dwarf pea vines (*Pisum sativum*) and smooth round vs. wrinkled angular seeds with yellow and green color variations.
- **Procedural Web Audio API**: Cell fusion pops, coin-flip gamete segregation clicks, and batch generation shuffles synthesized with zero external audio assets.
- **Observation Notebook & Probability Quizzes**: Record cross trials and practice inheritance probability calculations in randomized mystery challenges.
