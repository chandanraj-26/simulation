📚 Gregor Mendel's Laws of Inheritance

1. Law of Dominance

In a heterozygote, one allele conceals the presence of another allele for the same characteristic. The expressed allele is dominant ($T$), while the latent allele is recessive ($t$).

2. Law of Segregation (Monohybrid Cross)

During gamete formation (meiosis), the paired alleles for each gene segregate cleanly from each other so that each gamete carries only one allele:

P: Tt × Tt ⇒ Gametes: (1/2 T, 1/2 t)
F₂ Genotypic Ratio: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt (1:2:1)
F₂ Phenotypic Ratio: 3 Tall : 1 Dwarf (3:1)

3. Law of Independent Assortment (Dihybrid Cross)

Alleles of two (or more) different genes get sorted into gametes independently of one another:

P: RrYy × RrYy (16 Combinations)
Phenotypic Ratio: 9 Round Yellow : 3 Round Green : 3 Wrinkled Yellow : 1 Wrinkled Green (9:3:3:1)
