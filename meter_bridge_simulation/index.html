Wheatstone Bridge Formula

At null deflection ($I_g = 0$):

X = R · [(L_wire - l) / l]

Specific resistance (resistivity $\rho$) of the material:

ρ = (X · π · r²) / L_coil

Where $r$ is the wire radius and $L_{\text{coil}}$ is the physical length of the wire coil.
