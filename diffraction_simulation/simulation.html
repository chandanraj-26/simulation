Diffraction Law

Fraunhofer single slit intensity profile is governed by:

I(θ) = I₀ · [sin(β) / β]²

Where phase parameter β = (π · a · sin θ) / λ.

Destructive interference minima occur at:

a · sin θ = m · λ   (m = ±1, ±2, ...)
