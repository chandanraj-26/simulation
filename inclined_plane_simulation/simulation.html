Inclined Plane & Friction — NCERT Theory

1. Resolution of Forces on an Incline:
Gravitational force W = mg resolves into two orthogonal components:

  • Perpendicular component: W⊥ = mg · cos(θ), balanced by normal reaction N.
  • Parallel component: W∥ = mg · sin(θ), pulling the block downhill.

2. Angle of Repose (θc):
The minimum angle of inclination with the horizontal at which a resting body just begins to slide under gravity is the Angle of Repose. At this impending motion threshold, static friction reaches its maximum:

Threshold of Motion:
mg · sin(θc) = μs · mg · cos(θc)  ⇒  tan(θc) = μs

3. Kinetic Acceleration:
When θ > θc, the block slides downhill. Kinetic friction fk = μk · N resists motion. The downhill net accelerating force produces constant acceleration:

Kinetic Acceleration:
Fnet = m · a = mg · sin(θ) − μk · mg · cos(θ)
a = g · [ sin(θ) − μk · cos(θ) ]
