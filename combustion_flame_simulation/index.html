Three Flame Zones

1. Outer Zone (Blue): Complete combustion, hottest zone (~1400°C).

2. Middle Zone (Yellow): Incomplete combustion, moderately hot (~1000°C), glowing carbon particles.

3. Inner Zone (Dark): Coolest zone (~600°C), unburnt wax vapor straight from the wick.
